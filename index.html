About Me

Designer. Thinker. Learner.

I'm Samip Shah, a seventh-semester Bachelor of Information Management (BIM) student with a passion for design, technology, and creative problem-solving. I'm drawn to creating digital experiences that are both visually engaging and practical, which led me to explore UI/UX design, graphic design, and frontend development.

I enjoy learning through hands-on projects, experimenting with new ideas in Figma and code, and continuously improving my skills by turning concepts into functional designs.

Outside of design, I enjoy reading books, writing poetry, sketching, and exploring psychology and storytelling, as these interests inspire the way I think about creativity and human behavior. I also enjoy traveling, cooking, and staying active through fitness, which helps me maintain curiosity and discipline in both my personal life and my work. I believe that every experience, whether creative or technical, contributes to becoming a more thoughtful designer and lifelong learner.
